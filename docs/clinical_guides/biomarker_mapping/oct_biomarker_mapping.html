Disease-Level Interpretation Matrix

Diabetic Retinopathy / DME

  • DCP dropout
  • FAZ enlargement
  • Nonperfusion
  • DRIL (Disorganization of Retinal Inner Layers)
  • Retinal thickening & intraretinal fluid

AMD / Geographic Atrophy

  • Drusen presence
  • RPE disruption / atrophy
  • Hyperreflective foci
  • Ellipsoid Zone (EZ) loss
  • Choriocapillaris deficit

Neovascular AMD / CNV

  • Abnormal outer-retinal flow
  • Subretinal fluid
  • Intraretinal fluid
  • Pigment Epithelial Detachment (PED)
  • RPE disruption

Glaucoma

  • RNFL thinning
  • GCIPL thinning
  • Peripapillary vessel density loss
  • Macular perfusion loss

CSC

  • Subretinal fluid
  • Pigment Epithelial Detachment (PED)
  • RPE irregularity
  • Choroidal thickening
  • Pachychoroid pattern

Retinal Vascular Disease / RVO

  • Nonperfusion
  • Capillary dropout
  • FAZ distortion
  • Macular edema
  • Vessel density loss

INL: Inner Nuclear Layer
IPL: Inner Plexiform Layer
IZ: Interdigitation Zone
ONL: Outer Nuclear Layer
OPL: Outer Plexiform Layer

RVO: Retinal Vein Occlusion
